    Cylinder safety

    Simple habits that prevent most accidents.

    • Store cylinders upright, chained or strapped, away from heat.
    • Keep oxygen away from oil and grease.
    • Use acetylene upright only. Never lay it flat.
    • Check hoses and regulators for leaks before each use.
    • Close the valve and keep the cap on when not in use.
